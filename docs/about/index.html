About Us

The Wise Dollar publishes plain-English guides for people who are trying to stretch a paycheck, get help they are entitled to, or get out from under debt.

What we cover

Government and nonprofit assistance programs, household bills and utilities, debt and credit, and practical ways to save. Every guide explains who a program is for, how to apply, and what to watch out for.

How we write

  • We explain how things actually work, including the limits and the catches.
  • We point readers to the official agency or program wherever possible, and we never charge for information about public benefits.
  • We review our guides when rules change, and correct mistakes as soon as we find them.

How we make money

The Wise Dollar is free to read and is supported by advertising. Advertisers do not choose or review our articles, and ads are always labelled and kept separate from the article text.
