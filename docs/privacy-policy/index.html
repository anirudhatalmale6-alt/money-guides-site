Privacy Policy

Last updated: Nov 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Pocketwise ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, and how it is used.

Information you give us

If you subscribe to our newsletter or send us a message, we receive the details you enter, such as your name and email address. We use them only to send the newsletter or reply to you. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we and our partners receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed and the site that referred you. We use this to run the site, understand which articles are useful and measure advertising.

Advertising and cookies

This website shows ads served by Google and other third-party vendors.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We may also use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, which use cookies to report how visitors use the site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work without them.

Visitors from regions that require it are asked for consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used.

Sharing

We do not sell your name or email address. We share information with the service providers who host the site, deliver our emails and serve ads, only as needed for them to do so.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. To make a request, contact us.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
