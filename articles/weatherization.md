High energy bills are not always about how much heat you use. Often, a lot of that heat is leaking out of gaps around windows, doors and an under-insulated attic. The **Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP)** exists to fix exactly that problem, for free, for eligible households.

## What the program does

WAP is funded by the U.S. Department of Energy and delivered through state agencies and local nonprofits. A trained auditor visits your home, tests where energy is being wasted, and then a crew carries out the work that will save the most money. That can include:

- Sealing air leaks around windows, doors and the attic
- Adding insulation to walls, floors and attics
- Repairing or replacing an unsafe or inefficient furnace or water heater
- Checking for carbon monoxide and other safety problems

There is no bill at the end. The work is paid for by the program.

## Who is eligible

Eligibility is based on household income. In most states a household qualifies at or below 200% of the federal poverty guidelines, and households that receive SSI or TANF are often eligible automatically. Priority usually goes to:

- Older adults
- People with disabilities
- Families with children
- Households that spend a large share of income on energy

## Can renters apply?

Yes. Renters can qualify, but the landlord has to give permission for the work to be done. Some programs also ask landlords to agree not to raise the rent because of the improvements.

## How to apply

Contact your state weatherization office or local community action agency. You will normally need proof of income for everyone in the home, a recent utility bill and ID. There can be a waiting list, so it is worth applying even if you do not need the work urgently.

If you are also applying for help with your energy bill through LIHEAP, ask whether the same office handles weatherization. In many areas it does, and one application can open the door to both.

## Why it matters

Weatherization does not just lower one month's bill. It lowers every bill after that, and it makes the home safer and more comfortable at the same time.
